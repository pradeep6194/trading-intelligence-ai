FOUR BOOKS · FOUR PRACTICAL ROUTINES

What the reading contributes

Trading in the Zone — Mark Douglas

Think in probabilities, not certainties. Define and accept risk before entry; one outcome does not prove or disprove an edge.

Routine: judge a trade by rule execution, then review outcomes over a meaningful sample.
The Mental Game of Trading — Jared Tendler

Track recurring errors and diagnose the trigger, emotion, behaviour and decision that led to them.

Routine: tag each mistake and look for the same trigger repeating across sessions.
Best Loser Wins — Tom Hougaard

Build self-awareness around how losses and wins affect decisions. Do not let the need to avoid loss or recover money control execution.

Routine: accept the planned loss; never increase risk to get back to breakeven.
Trading Psychology 2.0 — Brett Steenbarger

Move from general advice to repeatable processes, deliberate practice and feedback on the specific skill being improved.

Routine: choose one execution behaviour to practise and measure each week.

These are original, concise applications of the books' broad themes, not book excerpts. Your reviews are saved only in this browser on this device.

COACH'S RULE

Risk is a decision, not an afterthought.

  • Define invalidation before entry.
  • Never widen a stop just to avoid booking a loss.
  • Options can lose value rapidly; gaps and expiry risk matter.
  • Paper-test a repeatable setup before risking capital.
  • Do not treat a probability estimate as certainty.
