How live data works

UPSTOX V3

Connected to Upstox Market Data Feed V3. The server authorizes the feed, subscribes to the selected instruments, decodes Protobuf ticks, and pushes updates to this screen over Server-Sent Events. If the market is closed or an instrument has no trade, its displayed price may not change.

  1. Connect your Upstox account securely.
  2. Confirm instrument keys and quote permissions.
  3. Backend reconnects automatically if the feed disconnects.

COACH'S RULE

Risk is a decision, not an afterthought.

  • Define invalidation before entry.
  • Never widen a stop just to avoid booking a loss.
  • Options can lose value rapidly; gaps and expiry risk matter.
  • Paper-test a repeatable setup before risking capital.
  • Do not treat a probability estimate as certainty.
